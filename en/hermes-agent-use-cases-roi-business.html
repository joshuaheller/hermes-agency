5 Hermes Agent use cases & ROI for growing businesses

The best Hermes Agent use case is rarely the most spectacular one. It’s usually the one with clear repetition, measurable time savings and contained risk. The following scenarios are especially relevant for SMBs, scale-ups and enterprise pilots — companies with grown system landscapes and stretched teams, from New York and Austin to Berlin and London.

1. Customer-service agent for recurring requests

Ideal for teams with high ticket volume, standardizable answers and a clear escalation logic.

  • Classify emails, tickets and web requests automatically
  • Answer based on FAQ, knowledge base and Salesforce/HubSpot context
  • Hand edge cases over to humans
ROI lever: shorter handling time, higher first-contact resolution, less backlog — and recovered revenue from faster responses.

2. Operations agent for documents and status reconciliation

Especially well-suited to logistics, energy, retail or industrial suppliers with lots of documents, manual status checks and recurring reports.

  • Read invoices, delivery notes or status messages
  • Reconcile data between portals, ERP (NetSuite, SAP) and spreadsheets
  • Spot outliers and flag them automatically
ROI lever: less manual coordination, faster cycle time, fewer errors.

3. Sales agent for qualification and prep

Helps teams that handle a lot of leads but rarely have enough time for research, personalization or follow-up — directly improving pipeline conversion and revenue.

  • Structure leads from forms, lists or emails
  • Enrich Salesforce/HubSpot data and set priorities
  • Generate personalized outreach drafts
ROI lever: more contact quality per rep, faster response, cleaner pipeline — and recovered revenue from stuck leads.

4. Finance & admin agent for internal service processes

A good fit for accounting, controlling or shared services where rules are clear, volume is high and traceability matters.

  • Prepare invoices and flag missing fields (QuickBooks, NetSuite)
  • Generate account reconciliations and report templates
  • Answer routine internal questions
ROI lever: fewer back-and-forths, faster month-end, lower error rate.

5. Internal knowledge agent for SOPs, policies and project docs

A particularly fast entry point when documents are scattered and expert knowledge lives in a few people’s heads.

  • Answer from internal documents, policies and templates
  • Cite sources and surface uncertainty
  • Take pressure off teams during onboarding and recurring questions
ROI lever: less search time, faster onboarding, fewer interruptions in day-to-day work.

How to calculate the business case properly

For a first prioritization, three numbers are enough: today’s volume, average time spent per case, and the expected level of automation. Looking only at model costs understates the leverage. The real value comes from saved handling time, lower error costs, faster response times — and the fact that existing teams can deliver more output (and more revenue) without growing headcount linearly.

Volume How often does the process happen per week or month?
Effort How much time does one case take today, including follow-ups?
Risk profile Which steps can run autonomously and where do you need approvals?
