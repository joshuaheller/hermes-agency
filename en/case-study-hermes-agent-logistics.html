Logistics Case Study

How a mid-sized logistics company saved 35 hours per week with Hermes Agent

The client was handling status inquiries and multi-carrier reconciliation manually across several portals. That meant high coordination overhead, errors in reports and avoidable customer follow-ups. The goal was not a flashy "showcase agent" but a productive operations agent for the day-to-day — live in 1 week.

35hsaved per week

Starting point

Three team members regularly spent a significant share of their week pulling data from different carrier portals, documenting exceptions and following up with customers. The problem was not just volume but fragmentation: multiple portals, different data formats and no central view of exceptions.

Solution design

  • Hermes Agent with custom skills for carrier APIs and status sources
  • Rule set for outliers, SLA risk and escalations
  • Unified reporting flow consolidating results from all portals
  • Human-in-the-loop for sensitive edge cases and manual review

The architecture was deliberately pragmatic: not a full autopilot for every edge case, but an agent that handles the routine and only pulls humans in where real judgment is needed.

Implementation in one week

  • Day 1–2: Process mapping, data sources, exception patterns and KPI definition.
  • Day 3–4: Tooling for carrier access, status normalization and reporting.
  • Day 5–6: Testing on real cases, sharpening approval logic and escalations.
  • Day 7: Go-live with monitoring, evaluation and tight feedback loop to the team.

Results after 90 days

The biggest impact wasn’t only time saved but better process stability. Status reconciliation became faster, reports more reliable and exceptions visible earlier. The team could focus on escalations, customer communication and process improvement rather than clicking through portals — and customer satisfaction (a leading revenue indicator) went up alongside the cost savings.

"The agent didn’t just replace clicks. For the first time it gave us a reliable operations loop: pull statuses, flag exceptions, report, and only escalate the difficult cases to us."
Head of Operations, mid-sized logistics company
